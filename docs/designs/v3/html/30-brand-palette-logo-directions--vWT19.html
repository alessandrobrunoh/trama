Woven T, two-tone
The original mark, kept. One strand takes the primary colour, so the two threads read as two parties, your team and its agents, crossing into one fabric.
LOWEST RISK · KEEPS RECOGNITION
02
Warp & weft
A literal 2×2 weave: strands pass over and under each other. Reads as fabric at any size and quietly echoes the grid of a task board.
MOST OWNABLE · STRONG APP ICON
03
Single thread
The T drawn as one continuous thread whose stem curls off like a loose end you can pull. Softer and more human; pairs well with a serif wordmark.
WARM · FRIENDLY AT SMALL SIZES
04
Running stitch
The T sewn in dashes. Each stitch is a commit, a comment, a handoff: small pieces joined into one line of work.
PLAYFUL · BEST FOR MARKETING
05
Linked loops
Two interlocked loops: the chat thread and the task it belongs to. The clearest picture of what Trama actually does.
