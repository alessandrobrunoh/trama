Naming · 6 candidates
Delta has threads.
What do you call the place that keeps them?
The product plans work, links one Delta thread per task and keeps the record forever. The strongest names extend Delta's own vocabulary — threads, rivers — instead of describing features.
RECOMMENDED
01
Trama
/ˈtra·ma/ · Italian · weft, and also plot
Weft is the thread woven across the warp to make fabric. Delta gives you the threads; Trama weaves them into tasks. In Italian it also means plot — the story of how the work went.
+
Already in use, short, fits the thread metaphor perfectly, the Italian roots give it character
−
Hard to own in search results (the word is common in Italian and Spanish)

Nodo
/ˈnɔ·do/ · Italian · knot, node
Every task is a knot: one thread tied to it, subtasks are knots on the same rope. It matches the one-thread-per-task rule exactly.
+
Expresses the 1-task-1-thread rule, easy to pronounce
−

Silt
/sɪlt/ · English · sediment a river leaves behind
A delta moves water; what stays is the silt. Delta does the work, Silt keeps what is left: decisions, summaries, changes. The strongest story next to Delta.
+

−
Sounds a bit raw, and the meaning isn't obvious without explanation
